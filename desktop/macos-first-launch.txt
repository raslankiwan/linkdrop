If macOS says "LinkDrop" Not Opened
====================================

LinkDrop isn't signed with a paid Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS asks you to
allow it once. After that it opens normally.

1. Drag LinkDrop into Applications and double-click it. Click "Done" on the warning.
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the message about "LinkDrop" and click "Open Anyway".
4. Confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click "Open Anyway" again.

Alternative (Terminal, one line):

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/LinkDrop.app
